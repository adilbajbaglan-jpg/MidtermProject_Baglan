BagaShop is a student frontend project. You can browse, save products, build a kit and place a demo order. No payment is taken, no personal information is sent to a server, and no products are shipped.

Add products to your shopping bag, then choose “Continue to checkout”. Complete the form on the Contact page. A valid submission shows a confirmation, clears the form and empties your bag.

Product prices are in KZT. Standard delivery costs 1,000 KZT and becomes free when your subtotal reaches 15,000 KZT. Campus pickup is always free. These are demo rules for the order calculator.

Your bag and saved products are kept in this browser with localStorage. They remain after a refresh, but do not sync to another device. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still shop during this page visit.

Each day type has a short list of useful products. The builder adds them in that order while staying within your budget. It shows the total and the amount left. You can review the kit before adding it to your bag.

About this project

BagaShop is an individual WEB Technologies 1 Endterm project by Adilbay Baglan, IT-2512, continuing the same store and four pages from the Midterm.

Code & assistance

Bootstrap 5.3.3 provides the grid, form components and utilities. jQuery 3.7.1 provides the FAQ, feedback, gallery and scroll effects. Both are distributed under the MIT license.

OpenAI ChatGPT / Codex assisted with the design, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, debugging and report preparation. Inline SVG icons were drawn in code with AI assistance. No downloaded website template was used.
